Frequently asked questions about PakServicesHub

How do I book a service? +
Browse our services, select the one you need, and click "Book Now". You must be logged in as a customer to confirm a booking. If you don't have an account, sign up for free — it takes less than a minute.
Are the professionals verified? +
Yes. Every worker listed on PakServicesHub goes through an identity and skill verification process before being listed. Verified profiles are marked with a 🛡️ badge.

Is PakServicesHub free to use? +
Yes, browsing and booking services is completely free for customers. Service providers may have listing fees — contact us for details.
Which areas do you cover? +
We currently cover Pasrur, Sialkot, Narowal and surrounding villages. We are expanding rapidly — if your area isn't listed, contact us and we'll add it.
How do I add my business to PakServicesHub? +
Contact us via the Contact page or call 0336-4254957. Our admin team will review your business and add it to the platform within 24 hours.
Can I cancel a booking? +
Yes. You can cancel a booking from your customer dashboard under "My Bookings". Please cancel at least 2 hours before the scheduled time to avoid any inconvenience to the service provider.
How do I pay for services? +
Currently payments are made directly to the service provider in cash after the service is completed. Online payment options are coming soon.
